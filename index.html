It's a lot to carry alone.

Most homeowners we talk to didn't plan to be here. A job loss, a medical bill, an inherited property, a divorce — the reasons vary, but the feeling is usually the same: overwhelmed, and unsure what's actually still possible. That's the part I can help with first, before anything else.

Your options

What's actually on the table

Every situation is different, and not every option fits every person. Here's the honest rundown — including the ones that don't involve selling to me at all.

Fastest

Sell before the date

A direct, as-is sale that can close in as little as 10–21 days — often the most certain way to stop the process outright.

Needs funds

Reinstate the loan

Paying the past-due amount to bring the loan current, if the numbers and timing allow for it.

Takes longer

Short sale

Selling for less than what's owed with lender approval — slower, but can avoid foreclosure on record.

No action

Let it proceed

Sometimes the right call for the moment. I'll never talk you out of this if it's genuinely where you land.
